The Songs

this is just an overview of some of my favorite songs from the musical and a description of how they fit into the plot.

Come Home With Me

this is one of my favorite songs because of Orpheus’s blatant refusal to listen to Hermes’ warning, “don’t come on too strong”, immediately asking Eurydice to “come home with [him]”.

We Raise Our Cups

this song is special because it is a song to the audience rather than a song that is part of the initial narrative. It breaks the fourth wall in a way that encourages the audience to become a part of the story and carry the optimism of Orpheus into their own lives.

Papers - Instrumental

I love this instrumental version honestly just because I think it sounds great musically, and it is funny to me that this beautiful piece overlays when Orpheus is getting beat the fuck up

Epic 3 (“They danced…”) - Instrumental

This is one of my favorite songs because it is the peak of hope in the musical. This is when Hades and Persephone finally rejoin each other in a dance because Orpheus has reminded Hades of the love he once held, and by doing so brings the world back into tune for this song

Promises

this song functions almost as a response to the earlier song, “All I’ve Ever Known”, where Orpheus and Eurydice initially decide to come together and love each other. This song reflects on that as Orpheus and Eurydice come back together and promise to again stay together no matter what happens to them in the material world, despite not being able to guarantee that they can give the other the things they promised them at the start of their relationship

Why We Build the Wall

this was my favorite song when I first went and saw the musical back in the beginning of 2023. This song ends the first act and puts the focus on Hades and his corrupt way of ruling the underworld.

About the Show

The show follows two love stories: Orpheus and Eurydice, and Hades and Persephone. Orpheus thinks he can sing the world back into tune, fixing all of the issues in the world of gods and men. Orpheus falls in love with Eurydice and despite promising to keep her safe, he is unable to hear her cries for help when he is trying to finish his song. Eurydice is lured by Hades into the underworld and Orpheus tries to go and get her back.

Quick Facts

  • Music, lyrics, and book: Anaïs Mitchell
  • Director: Rachel Chavkin
  • Based on the myth of Orpheus and Eurydice
